# Restock subsystem notes

Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-09-28 (#282). Edit here, not in CLAUDE.md, which keeps a one-line map entry for this file.

- **src/Restock.lua** — Restock-to-target engine. Pure target math (Option C: per-item target = `max(layoutDemand, reserve)`, `toBuy = max(0, target - stock - pending)` since #209; demand summed over display-tab templates, reserve from `GetStockReserves` (dormant until a producer ships), stock aggregated from the latest scan). `_RestockBuildItemUniverse` builds the displayed rows from the bank layout grouped by display tab (slotOrder-then-itemID order), number-key-coerced so a synced string-keyed layout still matches number-keyed stock; reserve-only items append under a Reserves group. **The group heading names the tab through `GBL:GetTabName(tabIndex, tab.name)` (#236)**, so it reads what the bank calls the tab today and falls back to the layout's capture-time name only while the client has none; the index is coerced first, since a synced layout can key its tabs by string. Per-guild-local settings (`restock.items` per-item overrides, `restock.budget`; NOT synced; guild-wide targets ride the synced bankLayout + stockReserves). Auctionator search+buy state machine (`IDLE`/`SEARCHING`/`READY`/`CONFIRMING`/`PRICED` on `self._restock`): `StartRestockSearch` resolves item names async then fires `AuctionatorShoppingFrame:DoSearch`, maps results back by itemID on Auctionator's `SearchEnd`; per-item and Buy next purchases (one per click, since `StartCommoditiesPurchase` requires a hardware event) spend real gold via `C_AuctionHouse` commodities: `StartCommoditiesPurchase`, then `ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase` on the `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_SYSTEM_READY` that follows `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED`, then `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_SUCCEEDED` / `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_FAILED`. Gold safety: affordability refusal using a lag-free spend estimate (GetMoney trails the purchase events), optional per-search budget cap, the wallet and the budget checked again against the priced total before the confirm, `confirmIssued` guard against double-confirm / duplicate-success mis-credit, Cancel from CONFIRMING or PRICED that drops an unconfirmed purchase at the auction house and keeps a confirmed one as the unanswered record. Every Auctionator/Item/C_AuctionHouse access is existence-guarded; pure helpers plus the fireEvent-driven state machine carry the unit coverage, live API paths verified in-game. (Catalog-free redesign: the earlier ported GBR catalog `src/RestockCategories.lua` was deleted in favor of the layout-driven list.) **The buy flow writes every auction-house event it sees to the system channel while a purchase is live (#199, v0.39.18), and that is the only log restock has.** `ahLog` writes `Restock AH: <event or step> state=<state> pending=<it:ID xN or none> t=+<seconds since the step started> busy=<yes|no> ready=<true|false|na> <detail>` through `GBL:SystemInfo`, AFTER the decision the line reports, so a line says what happened and never what was about to be tried (`busy=` is this flow's own throttle flag, below, and `ready=` is the client's `C_AuctionHouse.IsThrottledMessageSystemReady`, logged since v0.39.19 and gated on by nothing until a capture has shown what it reads around our calls): `start` with `via=next|row`, `skip` with its reason (the pre-start exits print to chat, and chat is not in the capture), `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED` with `unit= total=` and then `price in, confirm waits for ready`, `confirm issued`, `refused (<reason>)` or an `ignored` verdict, `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_SYSTEM_READY` with `confirm issued`, `ignored (already issued)` or `ignored (confirm waits for price)`, `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_SUCCEEDED` and `_FAILED` with `handled` (written before the pending fields clear, so it names the item), `handled (late, it:ID xN)`, `ignored (state=X)` or `ignored (unsolicited)`, `step failed` with its reason, `step done` with `next=N awaiting click`, `list done`, `budget reached` or `single`, and `reset` carrying the state the reset found and whether a cancel went out, which is the wedge's end marker when Cancel is pressed. The ten registered events are one exported list, `GBL._restockAHEvents`, looped by both `_RestockRegisterBuyEvents` and `_RestockUnregisterBuyEvents`; the spec pins a handler for each name because AceEvent errors on a registration with no method and the mock stores the name and checks nothing, so a dropped handler would be a Lua error on the first Buy click with the suite green. Two gates keep the capture readable. The events are registered on the first buy and dropped on reset, so idle play at the auction house logs nothing; and the throttle family (`AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_MESSAGE_SENT`, `_QUEUED`, `_DROPPED`, `_RESPONSE_RECEIVED`, `_SYSTEM_READY`) plus `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE` log only while a purchase is in flight (CONFIRMING, and PRICED since v0.40.0), because they fire for every addon's auction-house traffic and a player who bought one row and then browsed for ten minutes would push the run's own lines out of the 300-entry system capture; `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED`, `COMMODITY_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` and the two purchase results log in any state, since they answer a purchase flow. `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE` is in the set because TSM's wrapper treats `ERR_NOT_ENOUGH_MONEY`, `ERR_ITEM_NOT_FOUND`, `ERR_AUCTION_DATABASE_ERROR`, `ERR_AUCTION_HIGHER_BID` and `ERR_AUCTION_BID_OWN` as failure results of `ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase`, a confirm that fails with no `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_FAILED`. **The flow changed in v0.39.19 (#199 step 2), twice: the first cut on the code review, the second on the 2026-09-20 capture.** A commodity purchase is two calls: the start asks the server to quote the total for that quantity and is answered by `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED` (unit price, total) or `COMMODITY_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`; the confirm spends the gold and is answered by `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_SUCCEEDED` or `_FAILED`. Every auction-house request also goes through the client's throttle, which fires `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_MESSAGE_SENT`, `_RESPONSE_RECEIVED` and, about 0.2s after each response, `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_SYSTEM_READY`, for every addon's traffic. Through v0.39.18 the addon confirmed on that READY, which says the throttle is free and nothing about our price, and started the next purchase of a sweep inside the previous one's success handler, and Buy all wedged (the 2026-09-19 walk, #56 step 11). **Two rules came out of it, one per call.** The confirm goes out on the READY that follows the price: `st.throttleBusy` is set by every start and confirm and cleared by the READY after each response, and a confirm whose price is in waits for it (six of six confirms issued that way in the 2026-09-20 capture went out and succeeded; a confirm issued in the price frame when the READY had already come is the other branch and is spec-pinned but unobserved). **A start goes out only from a click.** [`C_AuctionHouse.StartCommoditiesPurchase`](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.StartCommoditiesPurchase) is documented `#hwevent` (a keyboard or mouse event must be on the call stack) and `#noscript`; called without one it does nothing and raises nothing. `ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase` carries neither flag. That is the whole of the swallowed second start: v0.39.18 issued it from the success handler and the first cut of v0.39.19 issued it from the READY handler, and the capture shows four of four handler starts producing no event of any kind against five of five click starts sent at once, so the "response frame" rule the first cut was built on was never demonstrated. **The structure is the invariant, not a flag: `_RestockBeginPurchase` has two callers, `StartRestockBuy` and `StartRestockBuyNext`, both click handlers, and `_RestockAfterStep` (reached from every result, refusal and timeout) settles to READY and starts nothing.** A spec fires every registered event and both timer outcomes against the start recorder and expects no call. Buy all is therefore **Buy next (N left)**: each click starts the first row `_RestockNextBuyable` accepts, walking past rows the pre-start checks refuse inside the same click (each refusal returns false, marks the row skipped under Buy next, and the loop ends on a start, on `buyAll` being cleared, or on an index it has already tried, which is what the no-API refusal needs since it marks nothing), and the tab shows the count from `_RestockBuyableCount`. **A skip carries its reason** (`st.skipped[i]` is `"max price"`, `"budget on this buy"`, `"cannot afford"`, or `failStep`'s reason such as `"budget at price"` or `"no price within 5s"`; readers test truthiness), and `SetRestockBudget` clears the ones beginning with `budget` when the value changes, because the 2026-09-20 run showed a single purchase landing at the cap and the next click marking fifteen rows skipped until Done and a new search; a bigger budget is the answer to exactly those and to no other reason. The banner shows the search's spend whenever it is above zero, with `of N g` only when a budget is set. A click that lands while `throttleBusy` (the 0.2s after a success) starts at once rather than waiting, since waiting for the READY would lose the hardware event; the client queues a throttled message issued while busy and holds the READY until it is answered (capture lines 79-82, Auctionator's own message), so the line to expect is `start ... busy=yes` then QUEUED and SENT, not yet observed for one of ours. The first cut's `WAITING` state, `sweepNext`, the deferred start and the wait timeout went with the deferral they existed for. The price event carries the real total, so the wallet and the budget are checked again against it there (the pre-start check is `minPrice x needed`, a lower bound), the spend estimate adds the priced total on success, and a price event with no usable total is refused rather than confirmed blind. **One step timer, `RESTOCK_STEP_TIMEOUT = 5` (TSM's `API_TIMEOUT`, exported), the repo's cancellable `C_Timer.NewTicker(n, cb, 1)` one-shot, bounds every wait, with two outcomes.** No price after a start: nothing was spent, so `CancelCommoditiesPurchase`, a chat line, the row skipped under Buy next and left buyable for a single buy, READY. No result after a confirm: the gold may have moved and a cancel means nothing now, so the purchase is kept as `st.unanswered`, the run stops, and chat says to check the mail, naming any drop or error the auction house reported meanwhile; a late `SUCCEEDED` or `FAILED` is credited to that record, because the events carry no purchase id, and nothing may start while it is outstanding. `COMMODITY_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` takes the first path (Auctionator's handling), a `FAILED` with no confirm out is ignored and left to the timer rather than stopping the run and blaming the current row, and Cancel drops an unconfirmed purchase before the reset and calls nothing after a confirm (`Dialogs.lua:27-31`, `Main.lua:50-56`). `UI_ERROR_MESSAGE` and `_DROPPED` stay log-only: none has been observed around one of our calls, and a safety action is not gated on a predicate whose behaviour has not been observed (the 2026-09-17 rule). Three readings from the 2026-09-20 capture to carry: `CancelCommoditiesPurchase` is a throttled message when the client has a purchase open (one SENT/RESPONSE/READY cycle followed the one cancel with something to cancel; the three issued after swallowed starts sent nothing); Auctionator re-searches the bought item on `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_SUCCEEDED` (`Source_ModernAH/Tabs/Buying/Commodity/Mixins/Main.lua:119`), which is the QUEUED/SENT/RESPONSE/READY after a success; and the events carry no item, so a foreign addon's price or result arriving while ours is in flight would be taken as ours. **One path no capture has shown** (the fast second click was observed four times on 2026-09-20): a refusal at price followed by a Buy next click, which reads whether a cancel issued from the price handler took effect; if the next start is not priced, the follow-up is a `cancelOwed` flag set by `failStep` and issued inside the next click, not built until that reading exists (the recipe is on #211). The expected good sequence per click is `start ... via=next busy=no`, SENT, RESPONSE, `PRICE_UPDATED ... price in, confirm waits for ready`, `SYSTEM_READY ... confirm issued` with a SENT in the same second, `SUCCEEDED ... handled`, `step done next=N awaiting click`. About eleven lines per purchase against the 300-entry cap means a reading run is two or three items or the head drops out; `lua scripts/audit-sessions.lua <path> --session N --channel system` reads it back. **Pending purchases are the first piece of the rebuild (#209, 2026-09-20).** An auction-house purchase arrives by mail, so the bank scan cannot see it until the buyer collects and deposits it, and a search in that window offered the row again (three rows bought twice on 2026-09-20). `guild.restock.pending[itemID] = { qty, unconfirmedQty, buyer, buyers, at, unconfirmedAt }` sits beside `budget` in the per-guild store (`getStore` backfills it, the Core.lua defaults declare it, `docs/DATA-MODEL.md` names it), local and unsynced. **`GBL:_RestockPendingParts` is the one place either quantity becomes a reading (#215)**, and the `unconfirmed` boolean the first cut stored is derived there rather than kept beside them, because two stored copies of one fact can disagree and nothing would say which was right; it also reads a pre-split entry (`qty` plus that boolean) as wholly unconfirmed, which is why no migration runs. Note the shape was written out in four places and the copy in this file's own subject, `src/Restock.lua`'s header, had omitted `buyers` since #209, so a reader had four statements and no way to tell which was current. **The split is what makes the eager park safe.** `st.unanswered` is session state, and until #215 it reached the store only through `_RestockSearchTeardown`, so a `/reload` before a reset or a new search forgot a confirm whose result never came and the next search offered the row again, which is the symptom #209 exists to stop. The two producers write the entry themselves now, flagged unconfirmed, and mark the record `parked`: `_RestockOnStepTimeout`'s CONFIRMING branch and `CancelRestockPurchase` after the confirm is out. `st.unanswered` stays as the session marker that blocks a new start and carries the late credit, so PR A's reason for not parking at Cancel (parking *instead of* the record left the row buyable) no longer applies, since both are set. A late `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_SUCCEEDED` settles the parked quantity into `qty` through `creditPurchase(..., { settle = u.parked })` rather than adding a second copy, and a late `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_FAILED` calls `_RestockReversePending`; a park the store refused leaves nothing to settle and the add is the fallback. `parkUnanswered` is the retry for a refused park rather than the first attempt, and it **keeps** the record when the retry is refused too, which needed the teardown's own unconditional `st.unanswered = nil` to go: that was a third way a purchase was forgotten with no line. What is left is a reload inside the roughly five seconds between a confirm going out and the step timer firing; parking at confirm-issue would close it and is declined, because it writes to SavedVariables on every purchase and doubles the `Restock pending:` lines in a capture bounded at 300 entries (#199's readability rule). `_RestockBuildItemUniverse` computes `toBuy = max(0, target - stock - pending)` over the **total** of the two parts and puts `pending`, `pendingAt`, `pendingUnconfirmed`, `pendingConfirmed`, `pendingUnconfirmedQty` and `pendingUnconfirmedAt` on the row, so the buy list drops a covered row and reduces the rest; an unconfirmed quantity still reduces `toBuy`, which is the conservative direction (never re-offer) and is deliberate. The IDLE row renders `in the mail N (2h ago)` and `N bought, result unknown (1m ago), check your mail` as separate clauses with separate ages, since one summed figure under the second wording said both parts were in doubt; a Clear button sits in the focus order (`ClearRestockPending`), the one interactive widget in that list, and it drops the whole entry rather than one part, which is why the row text has to say what it is dropping. **An entry the layout does not name gets a row of its own** under "In the mail (not in a display tab)", a third pass beside the Reserves group: the universe walked layout and reserve items only, so such an entry had no row, no Clear and no way to be cleared, and with the layout emptied it sat behind the "no items in your bank layout yet" message with no route to it at all. **The entry is cleared by the ledger and never by the bank scan**, which cannot tell the buyer's deposit from another member's: `_RestockOnRecordStored(record, guildData, opts)` matches a first-time-stored `type == "deposit"` record by player (any character in `buyers`, the `ResolvePlayerName` form `CreateTxRecord` writes; the store is per account, so an alt's purchase joins the entry and its deposit settles it), `itemID`, and `timestamp >= at - RESTOCK_PENDING_WINDOW` (3600; ledger timestamps are hour-coarse in a rounding direction nobody has recorded, so the window is tolerant and the stated cost is a same-item deposit by the buyer up to two hours before the purchase, stored after it, clearing early). **Each deposit line logs the record's offset from the purchase, `recorded +Ns after the purchase`, and that reading decides the window**: `ComputeAbsoluteTimestamp` is `now - hours * 3600` at scan time, so if the API floors the hours every real deposit reads at or above its true time and the window can drop to zero, which is the tighter rule; a negative reading on a real post-purchase deposit is the case for keeping it. Two limits accepted and written into the doc's section 6: a synced copy and the buyer's own scan of one deposit can land under two ids (the reason `CleanupWithEventCounts` exists) and the hook fires once per stored copy, and a late result after an entry was parked is credited to whatever purchase is in flight or ignored, the same window v0.39.19 had. **`StoreTx` passes `{ timestampRewritten = true }` and the hook refuses on it (#215)**, because `StoreTx` replaces an invalid timestamp with `GetServerTime()` **in place** before the hook runs, so no later reader can tell a record that carried this time from one that was given it, and a sync-received copy of an old deposit with a corrupt or epoch-0 timestamp (#93) would otherwise read as now, pass the window and clear an entry whose purchase is still in the mail. Refusing is the safe direction: a wrong keep costs one click on Clear, a wrong clear costs the gold again. `StoreBatchRecords` needs no third argument, since the scan path does not rewrite. A deposit drains `qty` before `unconfirmedQty`, because a real deposit is more likely to be the purchase whose result was seen and draining the other way would drop the flag while goods are still owed. **It has two call sites because two intake paths each store a record once**: `StoreBatchRecords` (`src/Dedup.lua`, scan; not gated on `storageKey`, since a money record carries no `itemID` and the hook ignores it, and a guard there survived its mutation) and `StoreTx` (`src/Ledger.lua`, sync receive). Not `UpdatePlayerStats`, which both call but which three migration rebuilds in `src/Core.lua` also call over old records. It reads `guildData.restock.pending` on the guild the record was stored for and creates nothing. Every store write is one `Restock pending:` INFO line on the system channel. The tab redraws through the existing `RefreshUI` after a rescan that stored something, and since v0.40.0 through `OnScanComplete` as well. **The flow half of rebuild step 2 (#211, PR A, v0.40.0) adds a fifth state and three rules.** `PRICED`: with the confirm-at-price pause on (`db.profile.restock.confirmAtPrice`, default true, `IsRestockConfirmAtPrice`/`SetRestockConfirmAtPrice`), `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED` runs its three checks (usable total, wallet, budget) and then, instead of confirming, enters PRICED with the quote on the banner and the one step timer re-armed at `RESTOCK_PAUSE_TIMEOUT` (60, exported; a placeholder until a run has read what the server does with a quote left for minutes). `ConfirmRestockPurchase` (a click, PRICED only) moves to CONFIRMING and issues the confirm at once when the throttle is free or hands it to the next READY when `throttleBusy`, arming the step timer either way, so a READY that never comes ends as `throttle never freed` rather than a wait with no timer. `CancelRestockPurchase` (a click, CONFIRMING and PRICED) replaces the reset those buttons used to call: before the confirm is out it cancels at the auction house (`dropUnconfirmed`), after it the purchase becomes `st.unanswered`, the record the result timeout keeps, so nothing starts until its result lands and the late result credits it; both return to READY with the list intact. **It is also parked as pending there, from #215.** PR A's review declined to park at that point because parking then replaced the unanswered record, which left the row buyable with the buy events still registered and let the late result land on the next purchase. Both are set now, so `_RestockRowBuyable` still refuses every row while the record stands, and the parked entry is the half a reload cannot lose. **A cancelled start's price is still on its way** (the cancel is queued behind the start's response), so `dropUnconfirmed` sets `st.cancelledStartDue` when the price was not in, the next price or price-unavailable is logged `ignored (price of a cancelled start)` and consumed, and the READY that ends that start's cycle clears the flag; the throttle serialises the cycles, so the next purchase's own price always follows that READY. **A price event in PRICED ends the pause** (`supersededQuote`: `ignored (quote superseded, pause ended)`, READY, no cancel, no skip mark): the events carry no item, so a second `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED` or a `_UNAVAILABLE` while our quote waits is either the server re-quoting or another addon's start, and a cancel would cancel whatever the server holds now. The design's re-price branch was dropped for this at the review. The pause timeout drops the purchase with `quote expired` and leaves the row buyable under Buy next too (`failStep`'s `keepRow`). **The Auction House window closing drops a quote or an unpriced start** (`_RestockOnAuctionHouseClosed`, from Core's `OnAuctionHouseToggled`): the server discards its pending purchase with the session, so a Confirm after it reopened would confirm nothing and end as a phantom unanswered record; a confirm already out is left to its result and the step timer. **A result in PRICED is someone else's purchase**: no confirm of ours is out there, and the client holds one commodity purchase at a time, so a purchase started elsewhere replaced ours at the server; `foreignResultInPause` credits nothing, drops the pause and says so in chat, which is the mitigation for the wider foreign-event window the pause opens (the events carry no item). Confirm passes our item and quantity, which the server is expected to check against its pending purchase; unverified. **The auction-house gate**: `_RestockAuctionHouseOpen()` reads the `self._auctionHouseOpen` flag Core's `OnAuctionHouseToggled` keeps from `AUCTION_HOUSE_SHOW`/`_CLOSED` (the direct signal) and falls back to `AuctionHouseFrame:IsShown()` (true under TSM's UI too, which shows the default frame at a tiny scale; the proxy for a client whose events have not fired yet). Search, Buy, Buy next and Confirm disable on it with the reason on the banner, and `_RestockBeginPurchase` refuses pre-start with `skip ... auction house closed` (marks nothing; a Buy next walk ends on the tried guard) ahead of the API check; before this the only guard on a Buy click at the bank was the existence of the API, and the step timer gave the row up five seconds later. **A blocked Search says so on the button itself (#217, v0.41.1).** Each `SEARCH_BLOCKERS` entry carries a `short` tag beside its sentence, and a disabled Search renders as `Search auctions (scan the bank first)` at auto width with the full sentence as a `GameTooltip` on hover; the banner keeps the sentence. Russell's call on the 2026-09-22 run: a greyed control that gives no reason reads as a bug, and the banner is at the top of the tab while the eye is on the control. The tag is text, so it needs no pointer, no colour and no second encoding; the tooltip is the mouse convenience, never the only carrier. **The preconditions are one ordered table**, `SEARCH_BLOCKERS`, read by `_RestockSearchBlocker(opts)`: `auctionator`, `ah-closed`, `shopping-tab` (today's wording until #194 moves the search to Auctionator's public entry, which selects the tab itself; the TSM row of the design's section 4 is not built until #194's observation decides it), `no-scan`, `nothing`; `StartRestockSearch` prints the first failing one's text and the IDLE banner shows it with Search disabled, so the reason on screen is the reason the click would print. **The shopping-tab precondition watches its frame (#217, v0.41.1).** The blocker is read on a rebuild and nowhere else, so selecting Auctionator's Shopping tab with the Restock tab showing left Search greyed until the tab was left and re-entered. `_RestockWatchShoppingTab` hooks `AuctionatorShoppingFrame`'s OnShow and OnHide once (a flag); both call `_RestockShoppingTabChanged`, which schedules ONE redraw on `C_Timer.After(0)` however many firings land in a frame (LibAHTab hides every Auctionator tab frame and shows the selected one on each selection, Auctionator shows its default tab twice on open, and the frame hides with the window). The redraw is held back only in a state that does not read the blocker (`_RestockStateReadsBlocker`, the one place the button, the banner, `StartRestockSearch` and the hook read IDLE-or-READY from; a rebuild in CONFIRMING or PRICED moves the focus from under the player, the #214 rule), and `RefreshRestockTab` decides the rest as it does for every other caller. Auctionator creates the frame on the first Auction House show of the session (its tab container's OnLoad, inside `AuctionatorAHFrameMixin:OnShow`; under TradeSkillMaster's scaled-down frame not until the scale reaches 0.5), so the install is lazy: `BuildRestockTab` tries it on every rebuild, and Core's `OnAuctionHouseToggled` hands a SHOW to `_RestockOnAuctionHouseShown`, which installs when the frame is there and otherwise, with Auctionator loaded, polls every `RESTOCK_SHOPPING_TAB_POLL` (0.5s) until it is or the window closes (`_RestockOnAuctionHouseClosed` stops the poll). **Two gates from the code review shipped in the first cut and were withdrawn on the in-game run, where Search never came back at all**: an in-view check on the cached `_restockInView` flag, and a compare of the blocker against the one the last build rendered. Each could suppress the redraw the fix exists for, and neither had ever been observed true on a real client: `_restockInView` is written only by `SelectTab` and read otherwise only by the wallet baseline, where a wrong value is invisible, so the suite's own fixtures were setting it by hand and proving nothing. That is the 2026-09-17 rule (never gate an action on a predicate whose real behaviour has not been observed) in a second subsystem. What replaced them is one `Restock tab:` line per install, per poll start and per redraw decision on the system channel, the last one only while the Restock tab is the active one (a line per Auction House tab click from a player reading their transactions is the idle-traffic noise #199's gates exist to keep out of the capture) and written after the redraw, so it names the precondition the build rendered: `redrew blocker=no-scan`, `=shopping-tab`, `=ah-closed`, `=nothing`, `=none`. **The 2026-09-22 run reads the whole mechanism**: the Auction House window opened at 17:45:55 and Auctionator's frame did not exist until 17:45:56, which is why the first cut's single next-tick retry never installed the hook at all and Search never came back; the poll caught it a second later; the Shopping tab coming on screen at 17:46:02 redrew with `blocker=no-scan`, the bank scan ran at 17:46:28, and the next selection at 17:46:44 redrew with `blocker=none` and Search enabled. It also read `inview=true` on the line that carried it, so `_restockInView` was innocent and only the compare stays unproven. The efficiency the review wanted (no redraw for a hidden window, none for an unchanged reading) waits on a reason to pay for it. The hook goes with the precondition when #194 deletes it. **The spend model (#60)**: `spentEstimate` (the priced total of each success) is what Spent and the budget read, and `_RestockSpent`, the wallet delta, is gone: it counted gold spent anywhere while the window was closed and greyed every Buy button against a budget the search had never touched. Affordability keeps a wallet baseline as a pair, `walletBase` and `spentAtBase`, so `affordableMoney` is `min(GetMoney(), walletBase - (spentEstimate - spentAtBase))`; `_RestockOnTabShown` moves the pair together when nothing is in flight, and only when the tab comes into view: `SelectTab`'s restock branch in `UI/UI.lua` calls it unless `self._restockInView` is already set, which `SelectTab` sets while the main frame is shown and `OnMainFrameHidden` (the frame's OnClose callback and an OnHide hook) clears. `RefreshUI` runs `SelectTab` after every sync receive and every rescan store, and that rebuild while the tab is showing can land inside the wallet lag right after a purchase, which is the moment the baseline exists to bound.
